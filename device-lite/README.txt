Skallywag Lite (free)
=====================
A live, color-coded activity terminal inside Ableton. Drop it on a track and send it
messages over UDP (port 11002) to see your scripts/OSC/automation in real time.

LITE limits: 12-line history; filter, pause and export are in the full version.
Full version ($19): filter, pause, export, unlimited history -> gumroad.com/l/skallywag

Setup: keep skallywag.js + skallywag-ui.js next to the .amxd (or freeze the device).
